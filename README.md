strike on the Crimea bridge, the Iran–US drone clash over Bahrain, the Quad
leaders' meeting in Manila, Nvidia's record quarter and its board ruling out

drawn programmatically with Python (PIL) in `draw.py` — masthead, headlines
and five small drawn illustrations, no AI-generated pictures — and `main.ts`
copies it to the entry point.
